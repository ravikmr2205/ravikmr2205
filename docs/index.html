A note from Tofu, your host

Woof! I'm Tofu

You're invited! I'm the youngest member of this family and the chief host of this wedding. My Ravi is marrying Priyanshi, and I have been given the most important job of all: making sure you come.

There will be haldi, music, rings and a lot of foodThere will be pheras, music and a lot of food I'm not allowed to eat. The celebrations may be a little too loud for my ears, so I might watch from a quiet corner at home. But my blessings and my tail wags will be with every one of you.
